# MindCheck Tools traffic and income deep dive

**Evidence date:** August 7, 2026
**Decision rule:** strict YMYL safety, rights, privacy, and qualified-review gates outrank traffic or revenue.

## Verified current position

- Search Console, May 6 through August 5: **424 clicks, 50,757 impressions, 0.8% CTR, average position 45.9**.
- Highest-click pages include the mental-load calculator (120), homepage (102), cognitive-distortion identifier (17), trigger-identification worksheet (15), and DBT crisis skills (11).
- Several useful pages already earn impressions without strong rankings, including burnout assessment, CBT thought record, DBT crisis skills, and worry-time scheduler.
- Search Console reports **963 external links**, all to the homepage. The largest sources are other sites in the owner's portfolio. This is not evidence of topical editorial authority.
- AdSense currently reports “Getting ready”; the live `ads.txt` is authorized. The code still keeps ads disabled under the site's stricter sensitive-health policy.
- Current MindCheck GA4 acquisition and revenue reporting was not authoritatively verified in this review. Do not infer traffic, conversions, or revenue from Search Console.

## Recommended monetization ladder

1. **Professional screening implementation readiness review** — use the existing bounded service page; accept no patient records, answers, scores, diagnoses or health data.
2. **Free authority assets** — publish and maintain the screening implementation checklist and source-linked instrument-rights guide to earn citations and qualify professional leads.
3. **Paid professional workbook** — only after demand evidence, create an original implementation evidence register template. Do not include protected instrument content or promise legal, clinical, privacy, accessibility or security certification.
4. **Institutional workshops or reviews** — pursue only after owner-approved pricing, contract, reviewer scope, and lead qualification.
5. **B2B product pilot** — consider only when at least five qualified teams describe the same costly problem and agree to a paid pilot. No patient or assessment data in an ungoverned prototype.

## Traffic plan

### Organic search and answer engines

- Improve pages already receiving impressions before adding more assessments. The best current opportunities are mental load, burnout, CBT thought record, DBT crisis skills, worry time, and source-comparison pages.
- Keep answer-first summaries, named publisher and reviewer roles, current primary citations, version dates, canonical URLs and structured data.
- Build fewer, stronger original references. The rights guide is designed as a citation target for health-tech teams, compliance writers, librarians and assessment-methods educators.
- Do not mass-generate condition, demographic or “do I have” pages. Google explicitly treats scaled low-value content as spam, and YMYL errors carry extra harm.

### Ethical link acquisition

- Earn deep links to the rights guide and checklist from digital-health engineering resources, university assessment-methods pages, library research guides, professional informatics groups, accessibility resources and relevant open-source projects.
- Government and `.edu` links are not a purchasable tactic. Seek inclusion only when the page fills a genuine resource gap and the editor independently chooses to cite it.
- Replace portfolio-wide homepage crosslinks with a small number of contextual links where audiences genuinely overlap. Never create reciprocal-link schemes.
- No outreach is authorized by this document. Each contact list, message and recipient requires owner review before sending.

### Partnerships and distribution

- Offer the free checklist to digital-health newsletters, behavioral-health software communities, health informatics educators and privacy/accessibility practitioners.
- Create one original quarterly update summarizing rights-term changes without reproducing instrument content. This can support recurring citations and email subscriptions.
- Seek interviews or guest contributions based on the documented implementation process, not medical authority beyond the named credential scope.

## 30-day measured loop after release

Track only privacy-safe aggregate events on nonsensitive professional pages:

- organic entrances to each authority asset;
- qualified clicks from the rights guide to the service boundary;
- scope-review email link clicks, without subject, message, diagnosis, assessment name or health context in analytics;
- referring domains and **deep links**, excluding owner-controlled portfolio domains;
- Search Console clicks, impressions, CTR and position by page and query;
- qualified inquiries and separately verified paid engagements.

Scale a channel only after it produces qualified professional visits or inquiries. Do not infer revenue, causation or clinical value from impressions, clicks or pageviews.

## Explicitly deferred

- AdSense or other ad activation on assessment, answer, score, result, crisis or immediate-help surfaces.
- Affiliate recommendations tied to a visitor's condition, answer, score or inferred health status.
- A therapist directory, hosted assessment SaaS, paid licence, sponsor integration, outreach campaign or price publication without a separate owner decision.
- Any new public instrument until rights, exact version, scoring, audience, crisis, privacy, accessibility and qualified-review evidence is complete.
