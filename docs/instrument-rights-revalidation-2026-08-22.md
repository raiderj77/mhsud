# MindCheckTools instrument-rights revalidation

**Evidence date:** August 22, 2026

**Scope:** 58 classified public tool routes: 27 distinct published/information instrument decisions and 29 original educational tools. Repeated PHQ-family routes share the applicable PHQ decision.

**Status:** repository evidence record; not legal advice and not a rights-holder approval.

No questionnaire items, answers, scoring keys, or user health data were reproduced during this review.

## How to read this record

- **Verified:** directly supported by the linked instrument owner, author-controlled page, government source, or publisher record.
- **Inference:** the conservative operating rule MindCheckTools applies to the verified evidence.
- **Unknown:** a grant, exact-parity artifact, or qualified review that is not in the repository.
- Every route remains publicly reachable. When public administration is prohibited, permission-gated, paid, noncommercial-only, or unresolved, public access means an indexable information page without the questionnaire, answer entry, scoring, or results.

## 29 original educational tools

The central registry labels these as MindCheck Tools original educational content, not reproduced published instruments. That is a repository authorship assertion, not an independent legal adjudication. Third-party quotations, images, frameworks, trademarks, and linked resources remain subject to their own terms.

All 29 remain non-diagnostic. Twelve are manually review-gated because their safety or health implications warrant continued human review. The Work Stress Reflection now uses a neutral visual scale and explicitly has no validated severity bands or clinical cutoff.

## What this review resolved

- Corrected the Athens citation everywhere from unrelated PubMed records to PMID 11033374 and the correct journal/DOI; recorded PMID 12932801 separately for cutoff evidence.
- Replaced the stale ASRS rights source with NYU's current v1.1 6Q terms and removed the now-unnecessary commercial-rights clarification gate for that exact version.
- Preserved DASS as a public, indexable information page while accurately distinguishing public-domain status from UNSW's prohibition on open-public electronic administration and respondent-facing automated scoring/interpretation.
- Removed implicit Work Stress severity signaling from color thresholds.

## Unknowns that cannot be completed inside the repository

These are external decision gates, not unfinished local research:

1. Written rights-holder approvals or paid licences for any future interactive SPIN, CRAFFT, SCOFF, MSI-BPD, AQ-10, Holmes-Rahe, UCLA, ECR-R, Athens, or WHO ASSIST journey.
2. Current-holder identification and written grants for CAGE-AID and Brief Resilience Scale; exact CDC-form selection for a future ACE journey.
3. WHO commercial permission, or a continuing technical and commercial segregation decision, for AUDIT, AUDIT-C, and WHO-5.
4. Named topic-qualified review of interactive trauma, depression/anxiety, ADHD, substance-use, pediatric, eating-disorder, autism, sleep, and other identity-sensitive journeys. CADC-II review is not represented as broader licensure.
5. Exact-form/scoring/attribution parity sign-off for every interactive published instrument. No such sign-off is fabricated by this desk review.
6. Owner authorization before contact, licence spend, merge, deployment, or publication.

Until those external gates are met, the conservative information-only/ad-free state is the completed safe operating decision.
