## Public accessibility re-check

**PRODUCTION ACCESS VERIFIED:** all 27 instrument routes below were fetched without authentication, cookies, or assessment input on 2026-08-05. All **27/27 returned HTTP 200**, none returned an authentication challenge, and none declared `noindex`. All returned `X-Robots-Tag: noarchive`; `noarchive` prevents a cached search-result copy but does not prevent public access or indexing.

**LOCAL INVENTORY VERIFIED, NOT DEPLOYED:** across the full maintained 35-route inventory, **21 routes remain interactive and 14 are indexable informational routes**. Within this 27-route register, **13 remain interactive and 14 are informational**. The informational set is DASS-21, SPIN, ACE, CAGE-AID, CRAFFT, SCOFF, MSI-BPD, AQ-10, ECR-R attachment, Holmes-Rahe, UCLA Loneliness, Brief Resilience, Athens Insomnia, and WHO ASSIST. Their questionnaire/scoring/result clients are removed locally, so each canonical URL remains publicly useful without administering or interpreting the instrument. The former DASS score/comparison and college-student wrapper URLs redirect to the canonical DASS informational page.

The owner requirement is that every instrument URL remain freely and publicly useful. This register does **not** authorize paywalling or login-gating an instrument. Where authoritative terms prohibit public administration, preserving an indexable educational route is the compliant public-access form. Its `BLOCKED` labels apply only to monetization/release controls, exact implementation review, and clinical wording. Public availability of a form is a separate fact from permission to reproduce it in a commercial context.

## Release policy

**ADSENSE ACTIVATION IS BLOCKED; PUBLIC ACCESS IS NOT.** Do not activate AdSense or another commercial monetization surface until every instrument below is either:

1. cleared with an authoritative, instrument-specific grant covering the exact items, scoring, public-web delivery, and commercial use; or
2. kept freely public behind a documented ad-free/non-commercial boundary that excludes the instrument from monetized page context.

A validation paper proves neither copyright status nor permission. A source-code claim such as "public domain" or "free for clinical use" is not a rights artifact. `BLOCKED` below is a gate for a new release, expansion, or monetization involving that instrument; it is not an instruction to deploy, remove, or change the current site.

## Status key

- **V - Verified:** directly supported by inspected repository evidence or an authoritative owner/publisher page.
- **I - Inference:** a risk conclusion drawn from verified facts; qualified clinical or legal review is still required.
- **U - Unknown:** the required evidence or approval is not recorded.
- **RL-BLOCK:** exact result wording has a known source-alignment or non-diagnostic-language issue.
- **RL-PENDING:** no signed, current, exact result-language approval was found. This is not a pass.
- **RD-PENDING:** the configured CADC-II reviewer is displayed, but a canonical exact review date and, where needed, topic-qualified clinical sign-off were not verified.
- **V LOCALLY:** verified in the current local source, but not deployed and therefore not proof of production behavior.
- **LOCALLY RESOLVED, RELEASE-GATED:** the production questionnaire/result defect is removed from the local route; aggregate release QA, owner deployment approval, and post-deployment verification remain required.
