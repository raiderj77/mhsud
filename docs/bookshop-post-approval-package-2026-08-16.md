## Release gate

All of the following must be true before a Bookshop link can be published:

1. Bookshop.org shows the account as verified or approved.
2. The exact storefront or list URL opens successfully in a signed-out browser and contains the correct affiliate attribution.
3. Every recommended title and edition passes the editorial checklist below.
4. The disclosure appears before or immediately beside the first commission-earning link.
5. The page is a non-sensitive editorial resource, not an assessment, result, crisis, safety-plan, youth, privacy, reviewer, or instrument-rights page.
6. The outbound link uses `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer"` and `referrerPolicy="no-referrer"`.
7. No merchant script, widget, pixel, iframe, retargeting code, answer, score, result, condition, assessment route, or user identifier is transmitted.
8. Accessibility, mobile, privacy, claims, citation, and signed-out production checks pass.
9. Jason explicitly approves the exact titles, disclosure, placement, and release.

## Required disclosure

Use this plain-language disclosure before or immediately beside the first affiliate link:

> Disclosure: MindCheck Tools may earn a commission if you buy through a Bookshop.org link. We choose resources independently, and affiliate relationships do not affect our assessments or results.

Do not rely on the words “affiliate link” alone. Bookshop.org says commission-earning links must be disclosed, and the FTC says the relationship should be clear, conspicuous, and close to the recommendation. See [Bookshop.org's disclosure requirement](https://support.bookshop.org/en/support/solutions/articles/65000168990-if-i-m-an-affiliate-do-i-have-to-disclose-that-on-my-website-newsletter-or-social-media-posts-) and the [FTC's affiliate disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking).

## Title and edition review

For each proposed title, record the following before approval:

| Check | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Identity | Exact title, author, publisher, edition, publication date, and ISBN from the publisher or Bookshop listing. |
| Author fit | Relevant public credentials and an accurate description of the author's role; no implied MindCheck Tools endorsement of a profession or treatment. |
| Claims | No cure, guaranteed outcome, diagnosis, treatment replacement, fear-based, or unsupported clinical claim. |
| Currency | Current edition and no superseding edition known; older foundational works need a clear reason for inclusion. |
| Evidence | Claims in MindCheck Tools copy supported by authoritative sources; marketing copy is not evidence. |
| Safety | Clear boundary that a book is educational and not emergency, diagnostic, or individualized care. |
| Audience | Stated audience matches the list section; professional resources are not presented as self-treatment. |
| Conflicts | Search for retractions, major corrections, licensing disputes, and material conflicts relevant to the recommendation. |
| Availability | Exact edition is available through the verified affiliate route and the link resolves correctly. |
| Accessibility | Avoid image-only descriptions; link text names the book and destination; cover images have useful alt text or are decorative. |

Reject titles that promote supplements, detoxes, cures, diagnostic devices, coercive treatment, unsupported equivalence between screening and diagnosis, or a book as a substitute for professional or emergency care.

## Privacy-safe measurement

Google Analytics, custom site events, and a consent banner are absent. Do not add a site-side affiliate-click event. Measurement is limited to provider-side aggregate reports that do not depend on a screening, result, crisis, youth, privacy, or other sensitive journey.

Minimum decision metrics after release:

- qualified organic visits to the editorial page from Search Console;
- Bookshop's aggregate attributed clicks, orders, and commissions;
- zero affiliate elements or Web Analytics requests observed on excluded sensitive routes.

Do not describe a click as a sale, a sale as profit, or Bookshop reporting as proof that a specific visitor used an assessment.
