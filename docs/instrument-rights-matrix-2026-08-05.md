# MindCheckTools instrument-rights matrix

**Research snapshot:** August 5, 2026

**Scope:** the 27 instrument routes in the current MindCheckTools rights register, plus a small candidate screen

**Status:** local research artifact; not deployed, merged, or published

This is an operational rights assessment, not legal advice. It does not reproduce any questionnaire item, response choice, scoring key, or other protected test content.

## Public-access policy and safety boundary

MindCheckTools' product requirement is that every instrument URL remain publicly reachable. Public URL access is not the same as permission to publicly administer, score, or interpret an instrument. If public administration is prohibited, licensed, permission-gated, or unresolved, the route should remain an indexable HTTP 200 educational page without the questionnaire, answers, score, cutoffs, or automated results.

**Verified local implementation, not deployed:** the maintained 35-route inventory is now **21 interactive routes plus 14 indexable informational routes**. Within this 27-route rights matrix, **13 remain interactive and 14 are informational**: DASS-21, SPIN, ACE, CAGE-AID, CRAFFT, SCOFF, MSI-BPD, AQ-10, ECR-R attachment, Holmes-Rahe, UCLA Loneliness, Brief Resilience, Athens Insomnia, and WHO ASSIST. Each informational route preserves its canonical public URL, citations, reviewer identity and credential scope, crisis and non-diagnostic language, privacy boundary, and useful differentiated alternatives while exposing no questionnaire, answers, scoring, cutoffs, or result journey. The operational "keep informational-only" state in the corresponding matrix rows is therefore implemented locally, not merely proposed.

No owner or rights-holder contact is required merely to release those 14 original educational information pages in their current non-instrument form. A future interactive implementation remains subject to the row-specific permission, licence, commercial-use, exact-parity, and clinical-review gates below.

The rights class below is only one release gate. An interactive journey also requires exact-version and scoring parity, source-linked result language, non-diagnostic wording, crisis QA where applicable, privacy QA, accessibility QA, and named topic-qualified review with a canonical date.

MindCheckTools should apply a stricter advertising rule than an instrument licence may require:

- No ads or ad-tech scripts on questionnaire, answer-entry, score/result, crisis, or immediate-help surfaces.
- Never send answers, scores, result labels, instrument selections that reveal a condition, crisis interactions, or inferred health status to an advertising system.
- Do not build ad audiences, retargeting segments, or conversion events from assessment behavior.
- Treat `A-NC`, `B`, `C`, `D`, and `E` routes as ad-free and noncommercial until a written grant says otherwise. A public informational route may remain available.
- An `A` classification removes the identified instrument-rights blocker; it does not override the no-ads-near-assessment policy or any clinical release gate.

## Evidence notation and classification key

- **V (verified):** directly supported by the linked owner, publisher, government, or author-controlled source.
- **I (inference):** a conservative operational conclusion drawn from the verified source.
- **U (unknown):** no adequate current authoritative artifact was found.
- **A:** public domain or clearly permitted for the contemplated public use.
- **A-NC:** clearly permitted only for noncommercial use.
- **B:** permission is required and a legitimate request route is available.
- **C:** a paid licence is required for the contemplated use.
- **D:** public administration is prohibited.
- **E:** rights remain unresolved.

## Exact owner/account-side approvals still needed

No contact, purchase, account change, deployment, or publication is authorized by this matrix. The following require a fresh owner decision:

1. **Current informational release:** no rights outreach or licence spend is needed merely to keep the 14 locally converted pages public, indexable, ad-free, and non-interactive. After the final aggregate local test/build/browser gate passes, the owner must separately approve merge and deployment; this matrix does not grant that approval.
2. **Future interactive permission outreach:** authorize each request separately before contact for SPIN, CAGE-AID, CRAFFT, ASRS commercial clarification, SCOFF, MSI-BPD, AQ-10, Holmes-Rahe, WHO-5, UCLA Loneliness, ECR-R, Brief Resilience, Athens, and WHO ASSIST. Approve the exact draft, recipient, disclosed site and revenue facts, and requested electronic/public/commercial scope. DASS public administration requires a written UNSW exception and should not be presumed available.
3. **Licence spend:** approve the quoted price, term, territory, traffic band, renewal, modification limits, screenshot/review requirements, and data/reporting terms before buying SPIN or any candidate licence such as PROMIS, SDQ, or ISI.
